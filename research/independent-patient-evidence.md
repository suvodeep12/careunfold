# Official Indian patient-feedback evidence: bounded public-source check

Checked 2 October 2026. Scope: Mera Aspataal and NHSRC NQAS/Kayakalp primary sources. Four focused search queries; selected official pages and programme publications inspected. No login, contact submission, raw reviews, patient records or paid service used. This is not an exhaustive claim that a suitable dataset does not exist.

## Decision

**No usable public individual-doctor reference dataset was established in this pass.** The strongest verified assets are a public patient-experience instrument and a hospital-level report archive. They help define questions or identify a potential facility comparator; they do not validate adjusted Google/Practo ratings for individual Noida doctors.

## Access, rights and comparability gate

The sources above were readable without payment. The verified output formats are programme PDFs, a questionnaire and report-index HTML; no publicly accessible doctor-level CSV/JSON/API was established. Public readability is distinct from an open dataset licence. No explicit instrument adaptation licence or dataset reuse licence was verified. The questionnaire is a possible freely accessible design reference; calling it freely reusable requires checking its rights first.

Mera Aspataal measures hospital-service satisfaction; the NHSRC form includes several facility services beyond a consultation. A common 1–5 scale does not make its target equivalent to Google stars or Practo recommendation responses. Treat any future hospital-level comparison as a separate target, with matched periods and documented sampling; never assign a hospital score to each affiliated doctor. No contemporaneous independent comparator for a named individual Noida doctor was established.

## Unavailable / partial checks

The Mera Aspataal homepage and attempted about-page fetch failed through the research tool. This is an access limitation, not proof that its public portal or dashboard is absent. The 2021 [NHSRC operational guidelines PDF](https://qps.nhsrcindia.org/sites/default/files/2022-04/Operational-Guidelines-for-Improving_Quality_Public_Health_Facilities_2021.pdf) exceeded the tool's fetch limit; search-index snippets were insufficient for a full methodology audit. No authenticated dashboard, underlying API or linked report PDF was examined.

## Next actionable gate

Keep the adjustment labelled a sample sensitivity scenario. Before adopting any public programme as a validation source, require one no-cost, attributable reference release with **individual doctor identity, exact patient-experience question, collection period, recruitment frame and invitation/completion counts, plus access/reuse permission**. None passed that gate here. The instrument can inform a future consented study only after recruitment feasibility, privacy and rights are resolved; recruitment or patient-data collection is not authorized by this note. With feasibility currently uncertain, do not build a collection pipeline or claim a validated rating.
