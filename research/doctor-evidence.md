No doctor receives a credential-verification badge from these checks. No doctor-level clinical-outcome dataset was established. Promotional case volumes, case studies, publications and hospital accreditation must not become a doctor's outcome-quality score.

## Evidence contract for implementation

Each claim needs a source URL, retrieval date, claim type, issuer and identity-match basis. Separate `provider-reported`, `registry-confirmed`, `unresolved` and `conflicting` evidence states. Match registration number and council wherever possible; a name alone is insufficient. Missing search results stay unresolved, and an inaccessible source never counts as an adverse finding. Show the scope and freshness of any status check, including the active/suspended filter.

A clinician-level outcome comparison needs comparable conditions/procedures, endpoints, follow-up periods, denominators, case mix and independent provenance. Unknown outcomes remain unknown. Patient-experience ratings retain their own sample and uncertainty; they do not fill that gap.

## Next acquisition decision

Use this pilot to test manual identity resolution before promising automatic registry verification. A supported data-access route and representative patient-experience evidence are still prerequisites for the final rating product. No live-Maps access or automated council extraction is authorized by a public page being readable.
